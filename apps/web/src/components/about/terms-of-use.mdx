# Guidelines

Last updated: July 22, 2026

## Purpose

pgko.dev is a community site for sharing user-created UMIGURI beatmaps.

## Affiliation

This is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by UMIGURI, its developers, or any related rights holders.

Using this site does not give you any rights to UMIGURI, or to third-party music, artwork, beatmaps, or trademarks.

## Uploads

Only upload content you created or have permission to share.

Please do not upload:

- Official beatmaps, or copies or modifications of someone else’s beatmaps without permission
- Malware, corrupted files, or deceptive files
- Unlawful, abusive, hateful, or otherwise harmful material
- Personal or confidential information that should not be shared

## Feedback

If something on the site infringes your rights or breaks these guidelines, contact support on the About page. Include the content URL and a short description of the problem.
